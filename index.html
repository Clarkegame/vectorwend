How to play

The grid hides several vector control words. The empty rows below it show how many there are and how long each one is.

  • Drag through letters that touch up, down, left or right (no diagonals) and let go to submit.
  • Words bend and wind, and every letter is used in exactly one word. Gray tiles are walls.
  • Found words lock in with a color. Stuck? Hint reveals the next letter and lights up its tile.
